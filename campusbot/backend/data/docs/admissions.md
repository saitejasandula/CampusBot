# Greenfield Institute of Technology - Admissions Guide 2026-2027

Welcome to Greenfield Institute of Technology (GIT). This document outlines the official admission policies, eligibility criteria, application schedules, and procedures for undergraduate and postgraduate programs for the 2026-2027 academic session.

## Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech)
GIT offers four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degrees in the following branches:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) - 240 seats
- Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS) - 120 seats
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) - 180 seats
- Mechanical Engineering (ME) - 120 seats
- Civil & Environmental Engineering (CEE) - 60 seats

### B.Tech Eligibility Criteria
Candidates seeking admission to the First Year B.Tech program must satisfy:
1. Academic Qualifications: Completion of Higher Secondary Certificate (Class 12 / 10+2) examination from a recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, or State Boards) with Physics and Mathematics as mandatory subjects along with Chemistry, Biotechnology, or Computer Science.
2. Minimum Aggregate: A minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the PCM subjects (55% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).
3. Age Limit: Applicants must not be older than 21 years as of July 1, 2026.

### Entrance Pathways and Cutoffs
Admission is conducted strictly on merit through two primary routes:
- GIT Entrance Engineering Examination (GIT-EEE): 60% of total seats are filled through GIT-EEE rank list.
- National JEE Main Channel: 40% of seats are reserved for JEE Main candidates. Expected cutoffs:
  - B.Tech CSE: 93.5 percentile or above
  - B.Tech AI & DS: 91.0 percentile or above
  - B.Tech ECE: 85.0 percentile or above
  - B.Tech Mechanical & Civil: 75.0 percentile or above

## Postgraduate Programs (M.Tech and MBA)
- M.Tech in Software Engineering, VLSI Design, and Thermal Engineering: Requires a relevant B.E./B.Tech degree with minimum 60% marks and a valid GATE score. Non-GATE candidates must qualify in the GIT Graduate Assessment Test (GIT-GAT).
- Master of Business Administration (MBA): Requires a recognized 3-year bachelor's degree with 55% marks, plus valid CAT, XAT, or MAT scores followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

- Phase 1 Counseling & Seat Allocation: July 5 to July 10, 2026

- Commencement of Classes: August 10, 2026

## Application Procedure & Fee
1. Visit the admissions portal at https://admissions.greenfield.edu.
2. Register an account, fill in personal and educational details, and upload scanned documents.
3. Pay the non-refundable application fee: INR 1,500 (USD 50 for international applicants) online via debit/credit card or UPI.
4. Download the confirmation slip and admit card when released.

## Required Documents
Applicants must present original and two attested photocopies:
- 10th and 12th standard mark sheets and passing certificates
- Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate
- Character Certificate from the head of the last attended institution
- Caste / Category Certificate (if claiming reservation benefits)
- Four recent passport-sized color photographs and government ID proof (Aadhaar / Passport).
