# Greenfield Institute of Technology - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Campus Life

This FAQ document answers common queries regarding student life, campus amenities, rules, administrative procedures, and support services at Greenfield Institute of Technology (GIT).

## 1. Campus Timings, Library, and Computing Facilities
**Q: What are the Central Library working hours?**  
A: The Central Library is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and on Sundays and public holidays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. During mid-term and end-semester examination months, the air-conditioned reading halls remain open 24/7. Students may borrow up to 4 books concurrently for 14 days.

**Q: How do students connect to campus Wi-Fi?**  
A: Connect to the SSID `GIT-CampusNet` or `GIT-HostelNet`. Authenticate using your student ERP roll number and default initial password (`Git@<DateOfBirth_DDMMYYYY>`). Network access provides high-speed 1 Gbps fiber connectivity. For connection issues, contact `it-support@greenfield.edu` or visit Room 104 in the Computer Center.

## 2. Residential Regulations and Hostel Life
**Q: What is the hostel curfew and in-time rule?**  
A: For student safety, residential hostels enforce a strict in-time of 9:30 PM from Monday through Friday, and 10:30 PM on Saturdays and Sundays. Biometric thumb verification is recorded at hostel gates.

**Q: How do students request overnight or weekend leaves?**  
A: Overnight and weekend out-passes must be submitted through the Greenfield Student ERP App at least 24 hours prior to departure. Parental SMS approval is triggered automatically; exit without approval incurs a disciplinary warning and INR 500 fine.

## 3. Healthcare, Emergencies, and Wellness
**Q: What medical facilities are available on campus?**  
A: The GIT Health Center is operational 24/7 with full-time resident doctors, nursing staff, and emergency medical supplies. Two dedicated ambulances are stationed round-the-clock for transfer to City Memorial Hospital (3 km away). Basic consultations, prescription drugs, and first aid are provided free of charge under student medical coverage.

**Q: Are mental health counseling services provided?**  
A: Yes. The Student Wellness Cell ("HopeLine") offers confidential psychological counseling Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM in Block D, Room 202, or via booking at `wellness@greenfield.edu`.

## 4. Transportation and Commuting
**Q: Is college bus transportation available for day scholars?**  
A: Yes. GIT operates an institutional fleet of 22 GPS-tracked air-conditioned buses traversing 14 city routes. The annual bus transportation pass fee is INR 26,000 payable at the start of each academic year.

## 5. Sports, Gym, and Extracurricular Life
**Q: What sports and recreation facilities exist?**  
A: GIT boasts an Olympic-size swimming pool, 4 synthetic badminton courts, 2 basketball courts, 1 floodlit cricket/football oval, and indoor table tennis. The multi-gymnasium is open daily from 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM with certified fitness trainers.

**Q: What are the flagship annual campus festivals?**  
A: GIT organizes two marquee festivals:
- `Technovate`: Annual international technical symposium held in mid-October.
- `Aura`: Inter-university cultural fest held in the third week of February.
There are also over 35 active student clubs covering robotics, coding, drama, music, astronomy, and photography.

## 6. Safety, Anti-Ragging, and Grievances
**Q: What is the college policy on ragging?**  
A: GIT strictly maintains zero-tolerance toward ragging. Any involvement in verbal, physical, or online harassment leads to immediate suspension and criminal prosecution under state law. The 24/7 Anti-Ragging Helpline is +91-1800-425-9988, and complaints can be emailed to `antiragging@greenfield.edu`.
