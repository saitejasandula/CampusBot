# Greenfield Institute of Technology - Academic Regulations & Examination Rules

This manual establishes the evaluation architecture, examination schedules, grading systems, attendance requirements, and disciplinary norms at Greenfield Institute of Technology (GIT).

## Semester Structure and Examination Schedule
GIT operates on a Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) across two main semesters per year:
- Autumn Semester: August to December.
  - Mid-Term 1 (CIE-I): Last week of September.
  - Mid-Term 2 (CIE-II): Second week of November.
  - Practical & Laboratory Exams: First week of December.
  - End-Semester Theory Examination (ESE): December 10 to December 24.
- Spring Semester: January to May.
  - Mid-Term 1 (CIE-I): Fourth week of February.
  - Mid-Term 2 (CIE-II): First week of April.
  - Practical & Laboratory Exams: First week of May.
  - End-Semester Theory Examination (ESE): May 12 to May 26.
- Summer Remedial Term: June 15 to July 15 (reserved for students clearing backlogs or pursuing fast-track credits).

## Evaluation Scheme and Weightage
Every lecture course carries a total of 100 marks divided equally between internal continuous evaluation and external final examination:
1. Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) - 50 Marks:
   - Mid-Term Examinations (Best of two tests): 30 marks
   - Quizzes, Surprise Tests, and Class Participation: 10 marks
   - Home Assignments and Mini-Projects: 10 marks
2. End-Semester Examination (ESE) - 50 Marks:
   - Comprehensive written test covering the complete semester syllabus.

### Passing Criteria
To pass a registered theory or lab course, a student must secure:
- A minimum of 40% marks in the End-Semester Examination (20 out of 50 marks).
- A minimum aggregate of 45% marks combining CIE and ESE.

## Attendance Requirement and Condonation
- Mandatory Attendance: Every student must maintain at least 75% attendance in both lectures and practical sessions in each registered course.
- Medical Condonation: Attendance between 65% and 74% may be condoned by the Dean of Academic Affairs strictly on certified medical grounds or authorized participation in inter-collegiate competitions. Medical certificates must be submitted to the Academic Cell within three working days of resuming classes.
- Debarment: Students holding less than 65% attendance under any circumstances are strictly debarred from appearing in the ESE and awarded an 'NS' (Not Satisfied attendance) grade, requiring re-registration in the next academic year.

## Grading System
GIT uses a standard 10-point Letter Grading scale:
- O (Outstanding): 90% and above -> Grade Point 10.0
- A+ (Excellent): 80% to 89% -> Grade Point 9.0
- A (Very Good): 70% to 79% -> Grade Point 8.0
- B+ (Good): 60% to 69% -> Grade Point 7.0
- B (Above Average): 55% to 59% -> Grade Point 6.0
- C (Pass): 45% to 54% -> Grade Point 5.0
- F (Fail): Below 45% -> Grade Point 0.0

## Backlog Clearance, Re-evaluation, and Unfair Means
- Supplementary Exams: Conducted every July and January alongside regular term exams. Fee: INR 750 per paper.
- Re-evaluation & Script Viewing: Students can request a photocopy of evaluated answer booklets within 7 calendar days of result declaration for INR 300, or apply for full re-evaluation for INR 600 per paper.
- Unfair Means (UFM): Possession of smartwatches, cell phones, or cheat chits in exam halls results in immediate cancellation of all registered exams for the semester, with referral to the Proctorial Board.
