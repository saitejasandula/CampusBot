# Greenfield Institute of Technology - Fee Structure & Scholarship Policy (2026-2027)

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the tuition fees, residential charges, miscellaneous institutional fees, payment deadlines, refund policies, and scholarship provisions for all students enrolled at Greenfield Institute of Technology (GIT).

## Annual Tuition Fee Schedule
Tuition fees are structured by academic program and are payable on a per-semester basis:

### 1. Undergraduate (B.Tech) Programs
- Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): INR 2,20,000 per annum (INR 1,10,000 per semester)
- Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS): INR 2,10,000 per annum (INR 1,05,000 per semester)
- Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE): INR 1,90,000 per annum (INR 95,000 per semester)
- Mechanical Engineering & Civil Engineering: INR 1,60,000 per annum (INR 80,000 per semester)

### 2. Postgraduate Programs
- M.Tech (All Specializations): INR 1,40,000 per annum (INR 70,000 per semester)
- Master of Business Administration (MBA): INR 2,50,000 per annum (INR 1,25,000 per semester)

## Additional Mandatory One-Time and Recurring Charges
- Caution Deposit (Refundable upon graduation/clearance): INR 15,000 (one-time at admission)
- Admission & Registration Fee: INR 10,000 (one-time, non-refundable)
- Examination & Library Fee: INR 8,000 per annum
- Student Development, Gymkhana & Cultural Activity Fee: INR 6,000 per annum
- Mandatory Group Medical Insurance Cover: INR 2,500 per annum

## Hostel Accommodation and Mess Charges
Living on campus is optional for day scholars but mandatory for outstation first-year students:
- Premium Double Sharing (Air Conditioned with attached bath): INR 1,20,000 per academic year
- Standard Double Sharing (Non-AC with common bath): INR 85,000 per academic year
- Triple Sharing Room (Non-AC): INR 65,000 per academic year
- Annual Mess Charges (Four meals daily: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner): INR 52,000 per academic year
- Hostel Security Deposit: INR 5,000 (Refundable)

## Payment Due Dates and Late Fee Penalties
- Autumn (Odd) Semester: Due on or before July 25.
- Spring (Even) Semester: Due on or before December 20.
- Late Payment Penalty:
  - Days 1 to 7 after due date: Grace period with zero penalty.
  - Days 8 to 21 after due date: Late surcharge of INR 200 per day.
  - Beyond 21 days: Temporary deactivation of campus ERP credentials and library checkout rights until clearance.

## Scholarships and Financial Assistance
GIT awards merit and need-based scholarships under the "Greenfield Excellence Initiative":
- Chairman's Merit Scholarship: 100% tuition fee waiver for top 10 rankers in GIT-EEE or students scoring 98+ percentile in JEE Main.
- Dean's Merit Award: 50% tuition fee reduction for students maintaining a CGPA of 9.2 or higher without backlogs.
- Need-Cum-Merit Scholarship: 25% to 50% tuition concession for students whose total family income is below INR 4,50,000 per annum, subject to maintaining a minimum CGPA of 7.5.
- Sports & Cultural Excellence Scholarship: 20% tuition concession for medalists at national or state recognized tournaments.

## Fee Refund Policy
Withdrawals submitted via the ERP portal follow University Grants Commission guidelines:
- 15 days or more prior to formally notified commencement of classes: 100% refund minus a processing charge of INR 1,000.
- Less than 15 days before commencement: 90% refund.
- Within 15 days after commencement of classes: 80% refund.
- Between 16 and 30 days after commencement: 50% refund.
- More than 30 days after commencement of classes: No tuition refund; only caution deposit refunded.
