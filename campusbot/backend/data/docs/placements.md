# Greenfield Institute of Technology - Training & Placement Office Report

The Training and Placement Cell (T&P Cell) at Greenfield Institute of Technology (GIT) facilitates summer internships, career mentorship, technical workshops, and campus recruitments. This report contains placement statistics, recruiter profiles, eligibility norms, and institutional placement regulations.

## Placement Performance Overview (Class of 2025)
The graduating batch of 2025 witnessed record-setting participation from leading international corporations and innovative tech startups:
- Total Eligible Registered Students: 680
- Total Job Offers Extended: 812 (including 132 multi-offer achievements)
- Overall Placement Rate: 94.2% across engineering disciplines
- Highest International Package: USD 115,000 (INR 96.5 LPA) by a Silicon Valley cloud provider
- Highest Domestic Package: INR 48.0 LPA by a Tier-1 Fintech major
- Average Package (Overall Campus): INR 11.2 LPA
- Average Package for CSE & AI/DS: INR 15.6 LPA
- Median Package: INR 9.8 LPA
- Total Companies Visiting: 164 companies

## Internship Season and Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs)
- Pre-Final Year Internships: Summer internships are scheduled during May-July (8 to 10 weeks) for third-year students. Average summer stipend for 2025 was INR 45,000 per month, with highest stipend reaching INR 1,25,000 per month.
- PPO Conversion: 142 students converted their summer internships into full-time Pre-Placement Offers before regular placement season began.

## Placement Eligibility and Registration Rules
To participate in campus recruitment drives, students must adhere to the following guidelines:
1. Academic Cutoff: Minimum cumulative CGPA of 6.50 at the end of the 6th semester. Specific premium recruiters require a CGPA of 7.50 or 8.00 without historical backlogs.
2. Backlog Restrictions: Candidates must have zero active (standing) backlogs at the time of recruitment registration. Dead backlogs (cleared prior to 7th semester) are permissible subject to company policies.
3. Attendance in Training: Mandatory 85% attendance in soft skills, quantitative aptitude, and mock technical interview training conducted during the 5th and 6th semesters.

## "One Student, One Job" Policy & Upgrade Tiers
To ensure equitable career opportunities, GIT enforces a tiered placement policy:
- Regular Tier: Offers up to INR 7.0 LPA.
- Dream Tier: Offers between INR 7.1 LPA and INR 14.0 LPA.
- Super Dream Tier: Offers exceeding INR 14.0 LPA.

A student who secures an offer in the Regular tier is permitted to appear only for Dream and Super Dream companies. Once a student receives a Dream tier offer, they may only attempt Super Dream opportunities. Securing a Super Dream offer concludes the candidate's placement eligibility, ensuring other classmates can access available openings.
